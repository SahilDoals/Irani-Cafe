Our story

Irani Café was opened by Persian immigrants in 1923, on a street corner where tram riders needed somewhere to wait. The marble tables, mirrors and wooden chairs are the same ones they set out that year.

We still bake in a wood-fired oven at dawn and still pour chai from a battered kettle. The menu has grown a little. The pace has not.
